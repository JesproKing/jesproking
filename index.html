About

I like making games that experiment with mechanics, atmosphere, and systems.

My name is Jesper Boersma. I am a Game Developer and IT Student from the Netherlands. I am currently in my last year and looking for a place to do my graduation internship.

I like making games, because it allows me to create many different creative things, like interesting systems, art, shaders, music, etc. I love creating and sharing my passion with others, and get a lot of satisfaction from seeing people enjoying my games.

My projects range from multiplayer party games and roguelikes to small experimental games.
